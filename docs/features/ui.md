# UI

All the UI uses UI Toolkit; no UGUI canvas is left. `UI/UI.prefab` holds the HUD, inventory, results and pause UIDocuments and the EventSystem; the main menu and the pre-menu splash have their own. `ChangeUI` gives access to them (`GameScene.UI`) and opens the menus from the input; `IsMenuOpen` tells whether a menu covers the game (pause, inventory, results), and the menus raise `ChangeUI.MenuChanged` (`NotifyMenuChanged`) when they open or close. `GameFlow` loads the scenes behind a wipe (`SceneTransition`).

## Assets

UI Toolkit assets live in `Assets/UI`:

- `PanelSettings` (1920x1080 reference, expand mode) with the `Theme.tss` theme, importing `Styles/Common.uss` (tokens, panels, buttons, sliders, screens), `Menus.uss`, `Hud.uss` and `Inventory.uss`.
- The screens in `Menus`: `PauseMenu`, `Results`, `MainMenu`, `Inventory`, `Splash`. The pause and main menus share the `Options` template, bound by `OptionsView`. The options are the language, the video (luminosity, contrast, fullscreen and vertical sync on/off buttons, `GameSettings.IsSwitch`), the audio volumes and the gameplay settings (screen shake strength, game speed from 1 to 2), saved by `GameSettings`. They are split in pages, one shown at a time under a row of tabs (`options-tab`, `options__page` in the same order: gameplay with the language, video, audio; `OptionsView.ShowPage`, the first page on opening), the footer's reset button resetting the shown page; each setting holds its name on the left and its control on the right, a slider followed by its value (`setting__value`, added by `OptionsView`: percents, signed offsets, speed factor). The options panel is wider than the pause one: the pause panel takes the `side-panel--wide` class while its options are shown and widens to uncover them. The pause menu shows a title above its buttons. Its Main menu and Quit buttons ask for a second click (`MenuConfirm`, `SecondClick` of `MenuScreen.cs`, which swaps the `key` of a button implementing `ILocalizedText` and adds the `confirm` class). The pause menu sets `GameTime.Paused`, freezing the actions, the enemy turns and the animations behind it; `GameFlow` resets the time when it loads a scene.
- The filters in `Filters`, referenced by `GameAssets`: the `SlantedBlur` backdrop filter and the `ArtifactPiece` filter animating the inventory pieces by rarity.

## Design tokens

The `:root` block of `Styles/Common.uss` holds every shared value of the UI as a USS custom property; the other style sheets declare no `:root` of their own. Write `var(--token)` rather than a literal whenever a token matches, and add a token (in its group, with a short comment) for a value that repeats or has a meaning; a value used once for a layout (a position, a panel size, a cut size) stays literal.

| Family | Tokens |
|---|---|
| Text | `--color-text`, `--color-text-secondary`, `--color-muted` |
| Accent and states | `--color-accent`, `--color-accent-light`, `--color-selected`, `--color-victory`, `--color-info`, `--color-valid` |
| Stats | `--color-health`, `--color-health-trail`, `--color-hit-flash`, `--color-shield` (also armor and defense), `--color-energy`, `--color-energy-text`, `--color-energy-empty`, `--color-attack`, `--color-movement` |
| Feedback | `--color-heal`, `--color-buff`, `--color-debuff`, `--color-warning` (a second press, a hit that may kill) |
| Overlays | `--color-overlay`, `--color-overlay-dark`, `--color-fade` (the wipes' opaque panel tint) |
| Panels and lines | `--panel-fill`, `--panel-fill-hover`, `--panel-line`, `--panel-line-width`, `--panel-line-width-bold`, `--panel-blur`, `--color-separator`, `--border-width`, `--sharp-shadow` |
| Fonts | `--font-display`, `--font-body`; sizes `--font-size-xs` (14), `-sm` (16), `-md` (18), `-base` (22), `-lg` (24), `-xl` (28), `-2xl` (36), `-3xl` (52) |
| Layout | `--space-edge` (48), `--column-side` (600), `--column-middle` (420) |
| Durations | `--duration-press` (0.1 s), `-fast` (0.15 s, hovers and popups), `-medium` (0.2 s), `-fade` (0.25 s, screens and banners), `-slow` (0.3 s, panels opening), `-slide` (0.35 s), `-wipe` (0.4 s, each way of a wipe) |

Code reads a UI color from a custom property rather than hard-coding it: `CutShape` reads `--fill-color`, `--line-color`... and `ArtifactPiece` its outline's `--piece-line-color` (`CustomStyleResolvedEvent`, `customStyle.TryGetValue`). The rarity colors are not tokens: they come from `RarityPalette`, shared with the relics (see [inventory](inventory.md#pieces)). The wipe of the HUD (`Hud.Fade`) and of `SceneTransition` takes its look from the `slanted-wipe` class.

## Layout

`Letterbox` keeps the camera and every document in the same 16:9 area. The HUD and the inventory share three columns: 600 points on the sides, 420 in the middle, 48 from the edges. A screen is shown by adding the `open` class. The `stretch` class of `Common.uss` fills the parent (absolute, 0 on every side): give it to an element rather than repeating the block in its own class.

Every screen script calls `MenuScreen.Setup(root, soundEmitter, sounds)`, which fits it in the 16:9 area, plays the button sounds of the `UISounds` asset (a moving slider ticks with the hover sound, every 0.08 s at most), staggers the buttons of its `menu-list` and uppercases the `caps` texts (USS has no text-transform).

## HUD

`Hud` (`Hud/Hud.uxml`) builds its panels, plain classes following the player and the events:

| Panel | Shows |
|---|---|
| `StatusPanel` | Health, armor, energy and the energy cost preview. Hovering shows a tooltip: the health bar its health out of the maximum and the armor taking the damage first, the armor's part of the bar (`HealthBar.Shield`, pickable while the player has armor) the damage it absorbs and when it is lost, the energy gauge its energy out of the maximum, what spends it and what refills it (`Tooltip*` UI keys). The energy gauge shakes, its empty cells in the accent color (`energy--refused`), when the player selects an artifact costing more than the energy left |
| `StatusEffectsPanel` | Attack and defense buffs or debuffs with their remaining turns, the icon punching when a status is applied to the player (`status-effect--pop`, `GameEvents.StatusApplied`); hovering one shows its tooltip (`StatusTooltip`, styled placement, right of the icons): its name, its change of the damage in percent from `StatusEffectData.delta` (`StatusDamageDealt`/`StatusDamageReceived` + `More`/`Less` UI keys) and its turns left, its line in the buff or debuff color |
| `LowHealthPanel` | A faint red vignette around the screen (`LowHealth` element, `Art/UI/LowHealthVignette.png` tinted by `--color-health`) while the player's health is at most a quarter of its maximum: it beats three times when the health falls that low and on each hit meanwhile (`low-health--beat`), then stays dim |
| `TimelinePanel` | The turn order; hovering an entity shows its tooltip (name, health out of the maximum, armor, movement points or the player's energy, status effects with their turns), outlines it and points the board at its tile (`Room.PointAt`): its tile, ring, threat and the selected artifact's targets react as when the pointer is on it, and clicking it casts the selected artifact on it if it is a valid target. The board's `EntityInfoPanel` stays hidden while the timeline points at the enemy (`Room.IsPointedFromUI`), since the tooltip shows the same where the player looks. A rebuild of the items (turn order change) ends the hover |
| `SkillsBar` | The artifacts of the inventory; clicking one selects it, hovering shows its effects (`Tooltip`, styled placement, above the middle of the bar); hovering and clicking play the buttons' sounds; a skill that can't be cast shakes when selected, or when a queued cast of it is dropped (`PlayerAttack.ArtifactRefused`, `RefuseShake`); a skill with [queued casts](entities.md#cast-queue) shows their count (`SkillSlot.Queued`, ×N, classes `skill__queued` and `skill--queued`), refreshed on `PlayerAttack.QueueChanged` |
| `EntityInfoPanel` | The hovered enemy's info (health, armor, movement points, status effects with their turns), above it, or below its feet near the top of the screen. The panel follows `Room.EntityHovered` (raised again when the same enemy goes from the timeline to the board) and the hovered enemy's `StatsChanged`, and, in combat while the player isn't aiming an artifact (`PlayerTurn.SelectedArtifactChanged`), marks the tiles the enemy can hit this turn (`EnemyAttack.GetThreatenedTiles`: its attacks on the player from every tile it can walk to; `Tile.IsThreat`, threat material of `TileOverlay`) |
| `DamagePreview` | Over each entity the selected artifact would hit (`PlayerAttack.TargetsPreviewed`): the health it would lose after its armor (`Ability.PreviewDamage`), and whether the hit is lethal or may kill, those previews beating every 0.4 s (`damage-preview--beat`) |
| `QueuedCastMarkers` | Over each target of the queued casts, their numbers in the queue (`queued-cast` labels in `Popups`), following the entity while it moves; an area's marker stays on its tile |
| `CombatPopups` | Over each entity: the health lost (red on the player, its scale growing from 0.8 to 1.45 with the health lost up to 40, the lethal hit 15 % bigger in the accent color, `popup--lethal`), the damage its armor took, heals, armor gained, status effects applied (`Status<asset name>` UI keys) and the score of a kill; the popups shown together stack, and the hits following each other on an entity within 0.6 s add up in its health popup, which punches bigger and stays longer |
| `BannerPanel` | Announces the combat start, the player's turn, the enemies' turn (once) and the victory in the middle of the screen, one after the other (`Banner*` UI keys) |
| `BossBar` | The boss's name and health at the top of the screen in its room, with a mark at its phase change (`DraregStats.PhaseThreshold`); the most health the selected artifact would take from it blinks at the end of the bar (`HealthBar.Preview`) |
| `MinimapPanel` | The map's rooms; `Hud` hides it while a menu is open (`ChangeUI.MenuChanged`) |
| `Fade` | The room transition: the `SlantedWipe` named `Fade` (`Hud.Fade`, read from the document so that the map can use it before the HUD's `Start`); `Map` covers the screen (`Cover`), loads the next room and reveals it (`Reveal`) |

`RefusalSounds` posts `UISounds.refused` when an artifact is refused and when a click on the board does nothing (`PlayerTurn.ClickRefused`, see [tiles](map.md#tiles)).

The action button follows `GameEvents.CombatStarted` (ends the player's turn) and `ExplorationStarted`; the deploy phase sets it through `Hud.EnterDeployState`. The `EndTurn` key presses it. Once the player has no energy left during its combat turn, the button beats (`beat` class toggled every 0.6 s, `Hud.RefreshEndTurnBeat`) until the turn ends. Ending the turn while an artifact can still be cast asks for a second press within 2.5 s (`EndTurnConfirm`, a `SecondClick`); the button's text is its `key`, following the language by itself. The skills show their key (1 to 9) and their tooltip gives the title, effects, range and cooldown. `Tile` ignores the pointer over a UI Toolkit element (`Hud.IsPointerOver`, any pickable element): the elements shown over the board (popups, damage previews, info panel, tooltips) are `picking-mode="Ignore"` down to their children, or the pointer would fall on them and the hover would blink.

## Components

Build the UI from the components of `Scripts/UI/Components` (`[UxmlElement]`, usable in UXML):

- `SlantedPanel`, `SlantedButton` and `SlantedLabel` draw the game's shape through `CutShape` (its polygons through the `PainterPaths` extensions of `Painter2D`, shared with `SlantedWipe` and `ArtifactPiece`): a rectangle with corners cut at 45°, up to parallelograms and diamonds, drawn as the element's background with a backdrop blur that leaves the cut corners sharp. A `SlantedButton` has the `slanted-button` class: display font, accent colors while hovered, pressed in; `outline-button`, `hud-action` and `hud-bag` only add their size and differences. Attributes `corners` and `dots` (the top line breaks in dashes next to a dot); size, colors, blur and sharp shadow from USS custom properties: `--cut-size`, `--fill-color`, `--line-color`, `--line-width`, `--backdrop-blur`, `--shadow-offset`.
- `SlantedWipe`, the screen transition: five horizontal bands cut at 45° sweep in from the left one after the other (a comb, not one diagonal) until they cover the element, then sweep out on the right. Drawn with the painter (`generateVisualContent`), no blur. `Cover(unscaledTime)` and `Reveal(unscaledTime)` are coroutines ending on the exact covered and hidden states, in game time or unscaled time, each frame counting 1/30 s at most so that the long frame loading a room or a scene doesn't skip half the sweep; it blocks the pointer while shown and is not displayed once revealed. Look from USS custom properties, set by the `slanted-wipe` class of `Common.uss`: `--fill-color` (`--color-fade`), `--wipe-duration` (a time, `--duration-wipe`). `Hud.Fade` (the room changes, in the 16:9 area, game time) and `SceneTransition` (the scene loads, whole screen, unscaled time) use it.
- `MenuButton`, `LocalizedLabel` (the `key` attribute reads the UI string table), `HealthBar` (health, armor, the trail of the health lost, `Preview(healthLost)` blinking the health an action would take at the end of the fill), `EnergyGauge`, `CostTag`, `SkillSlot`.
- `RefuseShake.Play(element, class)`, not an element: shakes an element sideways to refuse an action, holding the class (its refusal colors) while it shakes; the skills and the energy gauge use it.
- `HudTooltip`, a `SlantedLabel` shared by the elements registered on it (`Register(element, text provider, placement)`): hovering one shows its text after `HudTooltip.Delay` (400 ms); leaving it, removing it from the panel (a rebuilt list) or blocking the tooltip hides it. The provider returns null for no tooltip, and builds its text with `HudTooltip.Format(title, body, details)` (bold title, smaller details) and `HudTooltip.StatusLine(stats)` (the status effects with their turns, also used by `EntityInfoPanel`), and the owner calls `Refresh` from its events (stats, energy) to rewrite the shown text. An element hovered inside another registered one (the armor inside the health bar) takes over and gives the tooltip back when left. `Placement.Styled` leaves the tooltip where its USS puts it, `Placement.Above` centers it above the element, below it if there is no room, kept 16 points inside the HUD. The HUD has three: `Tooltip` (skills), `StatusTooltip` (`tooltip--status`) and `HoverTooltip` (`tooltip--anchored`: the stats and the timeline); `Hud` blocks them while a menu is open (`ChangeUI.MenuChanged`). A new tooltip registers on one of them (or a new `HudTooltip` in the UXML with the `tooltip` class) rather than handling the pointer itself.
- `ArtifactPiece`, built by code only (not a UXML element): an artifact's piece in the inventory, generated from its shape, rarity and icon (see [inventory pieces](inventory.md#pieces)).

A text element with children (a button with dots or a bar) is not sized by its text, in width nor height: `MenuButton` puts its text in a child label for that reason, and a `SlantedButton` or `SlantedLabel` with dots needs a set width and height.

## Style rules

- Blur only what is behind a panel, with the backdrop filter; never blur the whole screen (no depth of field).
- Keep the cut shapes at 45°, and make the hovers change colors only: a state selector (`:hover`, `:active`, `:focus`) never sets a margin, padding or size, or the hovered element moves its neighbours.
- Use the [design tokens](#design-tokens) rather than literal colors, font sizes and durations.
