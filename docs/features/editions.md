# Editions

The game is shown in one of two editions: the **Anniversary** and the **Classic**, the look and feel of the original release (`main`, v1.0.4). The gameplay, the data and the code are the same in both; the shaders and materials, the render pipeline, the level art, the ambience, the feedbacks, the UX aids, the audio mix and the UI's skin follow the edition. The in-game name of the Classic is "Originale" / "Original".

## Edition

`Edition` (`Scripts/Editions`) holds the current edition (`Current`, `IsClassic`), saved in the PlayerPrefs (`Edition` key, Anniversary by default). `Edition.Set` saves it (written to disk at once), applies the render pipeline and the materials, warms the VFX again (`VFXWarmup.Rewarm`), then raises `Edition.Changed`; everything else follows that event, so that the edition can change at any time, back and forth. Before the first scene loads, `Edition` applies the pipeline and hooks `SceneManager.sceneLoaded` to apply the materials to each loaded scene: a game started in the Classic never shows an Anniversary frame.

The options set it from the gameplay page (`Editions` row, one `SlantedButton` per edition, `Edition<Name>` UI keys, with a note under its name recalling the F2 key, `OptionsEditionShortcut`, `setting__hint`, hidden in the Classic, whose options had no notes), and the `SwitchEdition` key (F2, `Menus` map) toggles it anywhere (`EditionShortcut`, on `StartSettings` of `Managers/SETTINGS.prefab`, in the main menu and the game rig). Both go through `Edition.SwitchTo`: the change happens behind the wipe of `SceneTransition.Play(whileCovered, onDone)`, the one of the scene loads, which covers the whole screen above the menus and the pause; a switch asked while a transition plays (`SceneTransition.IsPlaying`) is ignored. A switch by the key names the new edition at the top of the screen once revealed (`SceneTransition.Play`'s announcement, `Announce`: `EditionToast` with `Edition<Name>`, "Édition Originale", the `edition-toast` label fading in, staying `--show-duration` 1.4 s and fading out; the Classic's a plain rectangle shown and hidden at once); the options' buttons don't, the options showing it already.

Nothing outside these mechanisms tests the edition:

| Mechanism | Use |
|---|---|
| `EditionProfile` (`Data/Editions/AnniversaryProfile`, `ClassicProfile`; `Edition.Profile`, `GameAssets.EditionProfile`) | The settings of an edition, for the systems that differ without being turned off (table below). The render pipeline is set on the quality level (`QualitySettings.renderPipeline`; none keeps the quality level's own); in the editor, the quality level gets its pipeline back when Play mode ends |
| `EditionSkin` (`Data/Editions/ClassicSkin`, `GameAssets.classicSkin`) | The Anniversary materials, prefabs and animation clips paired with their Classic counterparts (`EditionSkin.Classic(material)`, `Resolve(prefab)`, through which `VFXPool` and `Collectable` instantiate; `Current(material)` for code setting a material at runtime, which `EditionMaterials` doesn't see: the tile overlays' `TileOverlay`, which keeps its painted material and paints it again on `Edition.Changed`; `Current(clip)`, through which `EntityAnimator` plays every clip: the entity's override controller's, the attacks', Drareg's chained clip). What code instantiates outside `VFXPool` goes through `EditionMaterials.Apply` (Drareg's phase transition orb and chains, its countdown indicators). Several Anniversary materials may share a Classic one. A clip pair's Classic side may be empty: the Classic plays no clip there (`Current` gives null; an attack then runs over its `duration` without animation, an entity's state plays the base controller's clip). Written by the `classic-restore` skill from its `pairs.json`; empty lists are fine (no clip pair yet: the Anniversary kept the original's clips) |
| `EditionMaterials` | Swaps the `sharedMaterials` of every renderer under an object (particles and inactive objects included); a swapped renderer keeps its Anniversary materials to get them back. Applied to each scene as it loads, to each room as `RoomInfo` instantiates it, to each enemy (`Room` as it spawns an enemy layout), to each new pooled VFX (`VFXPool`), and to every loaded scene when the edition changes |
| `EditionOnly` | Activates objects, enables behaviours and renderers and plays particle systems in one edition only, on `OnEnable` and `Edition.Changed`: the Anniversary's additions, what only the Classic has. Put it on an object that stays active. Only for what can go without changing how the game plays |
| `EditionLight` | On a light the Anniversary changed: the Classic gives it back the original's type, intensity, range, color (`classicColored`), cookie, soft shadows (`classicSoftShadows`) and place, and a frame later again (a flicker turned off at the same time gives its intensity back). On the antechamber's crystal light (44.3, range 9.9), BossRoom01's two main lights (their soft shadows, which the Anniversary turned off for performance), CombatRoom01's fill light, the braziers' light (`ZLPC_Torch_06`) and the main menu's main light |
| `classic` class | Set on the root of each UI document by `MenuScreen.Setup` while the Classic is shown (`MenuScreen.ClassicClassName`), for the Classic's style sheet |

## What the Classic changes

- **Materials**: `Art/Classic` holds the original materials and shaders restored from main, each next to the place of its Anniversary counterpart (see [where the Classic's assets live](#where-the-classics-assets-live)) (the decor, rocks, cave, plants, water with the Bitgem water graph, tiles and their overlays, the crystals' `GlowBlue_Classic`, the enemies' glows, the player's glowing outfit, Drareg's weapon, the ability VFX materials on the 12 original Amplify shaders, prefixed `Classic/`). `ClassicSkin` pairs 100 materials (the player's outfit: the Anniversary's matte Character Glow fabric with the Lit originals, `*_Classic` in `Art/Classic/Models/Characters/Protagonist/Materials`, the Anniversary skirt's `GlowSkirt` with `SkirtTop_Classic`; the weapons' Weapon Glow with the original dissolve graphs, `Art/Classic/Models/Weapons/Materials`), the embedded materials of the models included (the life tree's bark, the wisteria's trunk, Kameiko's flesh, the bears' eyes).
- **Rendering**: `ClassicProfile` sets `Art/Classic/Rendering/URPSettings/UniversalRP-HighQuality_Classic` (and its renderer `ForwardRendererbab_Classic`), the pipeline the original shipped with (MSAA 2x, shadows to 50 m, 4 lights per object), whose renderer has no SSAO and only an `OutlineFeature`, white and 2 pixels wide like the original's QuickOutline. The camera of `Gameplay` holds a second volume, `Rendering/VolumeProfiles/ClassicGameVolumeProfile` (the original's vignette at 0.25 and motion blur), above the game volume, so that the luminosity and contrast settings still apply.
- **Turned off** (`EditionOnly` of the Anniversary): the flicker and snow heat of the torches and candles (`Environment/FireTorch`, `FireCandle`, `LightFlicker` restoring the light) and their low poly flame (`StylizedFlame`, `StylizedCandleFlame`), whose original particle flame an `EditionOnly` of the Classic on the prefab's root plays in the Classic only, on `Gameplay`, `DeathFeedback`, `BloodFeedback`, `RecoveryFeedback`, `ArmorBreakFeedback`, `TurnCameraFocus`, `CombatGrid` and `PathLine`; on `Player` and `Drareg`, `FootIK` (with the rings, the footstep dust and, on the player, the hurt audio, the `WeaponTrail` and `WeaponHold`, which holds the weapons in the hands and aims the gun: the Classic's hang from a finger, as the original's); on its camera and the main menu's, `WaterReflection`; in `Environment/Snow`, the Anniversary's snow systems, `SnowCover`, `RiftLighting` (which gives the lights back), `Wind` and `WaterSplash`, replaced by the original snowfall (`SnowClassic`); the Anniversary snowfall is stopped, not only hidden.

- **Entities**: GreatKameiko shows back its original smoke on its right hind leg (`Art/Classic/Prefabs/Entities/Enemies/GreatKameikoSmoke_Classic`, extracted from main's prefab), with an `EditionOnly` of the Classic; GreatNanuko had none. `EnemyGlow` (which stops its wisps), `EntityRing` and `FootstepDust` on `Enemy.prefab`, `Drareg.prefab` and `Player.prefab`, and the player's `PlayerHurtAudio` (the music's low-pass and the heartbeat), are Anniversary only. The models, rigs and clips are the original's, except the player's coat and skirt: the Anniversary's are copies skinned to chains of bones that swing (`GarmentSprings`, see [garments](entities.md#garments)), the Classic shows the FBX's (`Veste_Classic`, `Jupe_Classic`, an `EditionOnly` of the Classic on `Player.prefab`) and the springs are off.
- **Rooms**: each room's root holds an `EditionOnly` of the Anniversary listing its additions (the `GrassPatch` objects, the lanterns, the water drips, three plants the original didn't have, the antechamber's warm volume) and, where the original had cave-pack lamps (`ZLPC_Lamp_*`, `Lamp_01`, 9 in 6 rooms, replaced by lanterns), an `EditionOnly` of the Classic showing them back at their 2022 place. The pools keep their Anniversary cube with the original water material. The `room_diff.py` script of `classic-restore` lists the differences of each room.
- **Attacks**: the player's attacks reworked in the Anniversary ([attack rework](../attack-rework.md)) play their VFX of `Prefabs/VFX/Attacks`, paired in `ClassicSkin` with copies of the former prefabs (`Art/Classic/Prefabs/VFX/Attacks/<Name>_Classic`); their timing is the profile's `attackTiming`. Their marks on the ground (`Ground` child and `VFXLifetime`) exist in the Anniversary prefabs only: the Classic's copies leave nothing on the ground. An Anniversary prefab that starts from another anchor than the original carries a `VFXAnchor` (see [combat](combat.md)), so that the ability's `VFXInfo` target stays the Classic's.
- **Exits**: `ClassicSkin` pairs the Anniversary's procedural portal (`Prefabs/VFX/RoomExit`) with the original's particles (`Art/Classic/Prefabs/VFX/RoomExit_Classic`, nesting `RoomExitVFX_Classic`), which `TransitionTile` instantiates through `EditionSkin.Resolve` and swaps on `Edition.Changed`: no reveal, no color by destination, no hover (`ExitPortal` is the Anniversary's), and the minimap's rooms keep the original's look (see [UI](ui.md)).
- **Drops**: `ClassicSkin` pairs the four drop auras with the original ones (`Art/Classic/Prefabs/VFX/Drop`, `CommonDrop_Classic` to `LegendaryDrop_Classic` on `vfxGraph_Drop.vfx`); `Collectable` instantiates the edition's aura and swaps it on `Edition.Changed`. The hover answer (`RelicHover`: the orb steadying and lighting up, the burst of fragments, `Relic_Hover`) is on the Anniversary's aura only, so the Classic's relic stays still under the pointer, as the original's.
- **UI**: the Classic sheets, every rule under `.classic`, put on the roots of the screens by `ClassicStyles` while the Classic is shown (addressable, group `ClassicUI`, listed in `GameAssets.classicSheets`: nothing the Anniversary loads references them, so their textures are only loaded in the Classic and released when switching back), give the screens the original's look over the same UXML: `Styles/Classic.uss` holds the tokens and the shared rules (fonts, button states, rectangles instead of cut shapes, what the original didn't show), then `ClassicHud.uss`, `ClassicMenus.uss` (main menu, options, credits, disclaimer, pause, results) and `ClassicInventory.uss` each area's. The original's texts: most carried a `LocalizedUITextSetter` that replaced the whole text, so their serialized tags (`<i>`, `<font-weight>`) never showed; only the component's font and style flags did (the pause and main menu buttons are upright Kallisto Bold, `--classic-font-bold`), while the texts set from code kept their tags.
  - **Tokens**: the original's fonts (Kallisto Medium, Bicyclette), plain dark panels without blur or line, rectangles instead of cut shapes (`--cut-size: 0`), `--ui-effects: 0` (no animated filters on the health bar and the inventory pieces). The pink accent stays; the buttons take the original's states from its UGUI buttons (`--classic-hover` lilac #9D9BBA, `--classic-pressed`, `--classic-pressed-hud` red #D22F45 on the end turn and bag buttons, `--classic-hover-hud` grey on the end turn button, `--classic-focus` near white on a selected one); the lighter blurs `--panel-blur-button` and `--panel-blur-icon` are 0 too. The original's other colors are `--classic-*` tokens too, so that they don't follow the Anniversary's: texts, lines and captions (`--classic-text`, `-text-secondary`, `-line`, `-caption`), sprite tints (`-untinted`, `-dimmed`, `-faded`), the selected skill and the stats (`-selected`, `-health`, `-movement`), the disclaimer's button (`-blue`, `-blue-hover`, `-blue-pressed`), the backdrops (`-black`, `-backdrop-pause`, `-backdrop-results`) and the results' message (`-defeat`, `-victory`); the style sheets write no literal color.
  - **Sprites** (`Art/Classic/UI`, in `HUD`, `Inventory` and `Menus` like the sheets): end turn and bag buttons, skills (the selected one tinted purple, an unusable one's icon greyed), cost tags, cooldown ring, energy cells (full, empty, previewed), health and armor bars (9-sliced), status and timeline panels, enemy info, tooltips, minimap background and rooms, pause panel, results buttons, the options' footer buttons and sliders, the inventory and chest panels.
  - **No animation the original didn't have**: the health bar's flash, trail and tweens, the energy cells', skills' and status icons' pops, the skills' hover lift, the timeline's dimming, growth and marker, the tooltips' and enemy info's fades and slides, the minimap's slide, the menus' staggered slide-in and hover bar, the pause title, dialog and results slide-ins; the damage numbers fade in place. The pause's panel slides in over 0.167 s without fading while only its black backdrop fades to 0.8; on close the black and the buttons go at once and the empty panel slides out (`#Pause.classic` in `ClassicMenus.uss`). The credits scroll 3292 points in 35 s, easing in and out, their title with them, as the original's `Credits.anim`; the inventory panels scale in over 0.125 s, the results show on black 0.9 with the original's sizes and defeat and victory colors.
  - **Inventory pieces**: `ArtifactData.classicInventorySprite` references the original's sprite of each piece (`Art/Classic/UI/Inventory/Pieces`, its shape and colors baked in; an `AssetReferenceSprite` in the `ClassicUI` group); `ArtifactPiece` lays an element over the piece, hidden, whose image `ClassicStyles.Image` loads in the Classic only, and `ClassicInventory.uss` shows it instead of the drawn cells and outline (`--piece-drawn: 0`). The components read their custom properties (`--ui-effects`, `--piece-drawn`, `--tooltip-stat-color`) on themselves: the Classic sheets set them on `.health-bar`, `.artifact-piece` and `.tooltip--anchored`, since `customStyle` doesn't see the inherited ones.
  - **Hidden**: low health vignette, banners, boss bar, damage preview, queued casts, the enemy's armor and statuses.
  - **Layout**: the original's canvases were 1920x1080 like our panels, so the Classic sheets place the elements at the original's rects, measured from main's prefabs and scenes: the HUD (status panel, its health text in three labels as the original's, `health-bar__split`; end turn button, timeline, skills, spaced by their count as the original's, `skills--count-N` of `SkillsBar`; tooltips, buffs, bag, minimap, enemy panel), the inventory (panels, character sheet, artifact details; the empty slots wear the original's `Case_test.png`, restored in `Art/Classic/UI/Inventory`; the grid and the hand layer are scaled by 1.125 to the original's 90 point cells, the drag and drop converting through `WorldToLocal`/`LocalToWorld`, which include the scale; the cost and cooldown show their hover labels, as the original's `HoverActivator`), the pause's main page, the results, the main menu, the credits and the disclaimer. The options take the original's pages: the main menu's full screen over the scene (title, 1660 point lines, centered settings with their name above), the pause's 688 point panel with its right-aligned settings; our tabs stand for the original's section titles (Video, Audio), with one reset button in the footer instead of one per section, and the original's sprites keep their proportions (`scale-to-fit`, as its `preserveAspect`). The splash keeps ours (the original's pre-menu was a text page).
- **Audio**: the music and the samples are the same. `EditionMix` (on `StartSettings` of `Managers/SETTINGS.prefab`) sets the `Edition` game parameter, which bypasses the `ImpactMeter` of the `Impacts` bus in the Classic: no duck of the music on the hits. It also sets the `Edition` state, on which the attack sounds trimmed for the Anniversary's timing keep their original in the Classic, and the Classic plays the attacks' sounds without their `soundDelay` (`attackSoundDelay`, see [attack sounds](../tech/audio.md#attack-sounds)). The Classic keeps the original's single ambience everywhere (`placeAmbience` off, see [ambience](../tech/audio.md#ambience)); the finisher's sound follows `impactFeedback`.

## Maintaining both editions

Every visual, feedback or UX change of the Anniversary decides its Classic answer in the same commit. The Classic is the original release: what the original didn't have is off in the Classic, what it had keeps its original look.

### Adding or changing a feature

| Change | Classic answer |
|---|---|
| A new object, VFX, ambience or level art piece | Put it on an object listed in the `objects` of an `EditionOnly` of the Anniversary (for level art, the room's `Anniversary` group, which the root's `EditionOnly` lists: see [room hierarchy](map.md#room-hierarchy); the prefab's root for an effect). Deactivating the object is the cheapest answer: nothing updates, simulates or renders |
| A new behaviour on an object both editions keep | List it in `behaviours`. Its `OnDisable` must undo what it did (lights, camera, property blocks, global shader values), hide what it spawned as separate objects, and its animation event methods must check `enabled` (the events reach disabled components) |
| A particle system or renderer on an object that must stay active | `particleSystems` (stopped and cleared, no simulation) and `renderers` |
| A system changing how a step of the game feels (timings, what is shown, input) | A setting of `EditionProfile`, filled in both profiles, read where it applies; never a test of the edition |
| A material, shader or texture changed on an asset that existed on main | It changes the Classic too. Pair the Anniversary asset with the original (`restore.py`, `pairs.json`, `organize.py`, `build_skin.py`); a material shared by objects that had different originals is split first |
| A VFX prefab of an ability changed (new particles, timing, meshes) | The prefab is shared: an object added only for the Anniversary goes under an `EditionOnly`; when the whole effect is redone, pair the prefab with the original's (`prefabs` of `pairs.json`: the pooled VFX and the drops go through `EditionSkin.Resolve`) |
| An animation clip changed, or an ability or entity given a new clip | Pair the new clip with the one the original played (`clips` of `pairs.json`, then `build_skin.py`): `EntityAnimator` plays the original's in the Classic, for the entity's override controller's clips and the attacks alike. A clip for something the original didn't have needs nothing, unless that thing played no clip in the original (an attack without animation, as Rush or HitBuff): pair it with `null` so that the Classic keeps playing none. Edit a copy rather than the original clip in place, or restore the original under `Art/Classic` first (`restore.py`) |
| A new USS animation, transition, decoration or element | Its rule under `.classic` in the Classic sheet of its area (`ClassicHud.uss`, `ClassicMenus.uss`, `ClassicInventory.uss`; `Classic.uss` for a shared component), neutralized or hidden. A style set from C# is inline and beats USS: read a custom property instead (`--ui-effects`) or a profile setting |
| A new screen or HUD panel the original didn't have | Hidden under `.classic`, or dressed with the tokens if it is needed to play |
| A gameplay change or a bug fix | Nothing: both editions share it |

Before committing, `coverage.py` of the `classic-restore` skill lists the Anniversary's materials, animation clips and visual scripts without a Classic answer (it scans the prefabs of `Prefabs/Rooms`, `Entities`, `Environment`, `VFX`, `LevelDesign` and `Managers`: not the scenes, nor the materials code assigns at runtime; these gaps only report, they never fail it), and the broken edition references: an `EditionOnly` entry or a `ClassicSkin` side pointing to nothing, which the runtime skips without an error (the Anniversary's look comes back in the Classic). The docs gate hook runs it and blocks a commit leaving one, even with `DOCS_REVIEWED=1`. In Play mode, `Editions.Flip` of `unity-playtest` checks that a switch there and back gives every renderer its materials back and that the Classic shows no paired Anniversary material.

### Where the Classic's assets live

Everything only the Classic uses is under `Assets/Art/Classic`, laid out by `organize.py` (`classic-restore`) so that each asset's Classic side is found from the Anniversary's, and never in the Anniversary's folders:

| Asset | Place and name |
|---|---|
| The Classic side of a pair (material, prefab, clip) or of a counterpart the profile or a prefab names (the pipeline, the snowfall) | The Anniversary asset's folder mirrored under `Art/Classic` (`Assets/Art/X` becomes `Art/Classic/X`, `Assets/X` outside `Art` becomes `Art/Classic/X`), its name followed by `_Classic`: `Art/Materials/Environment/Mat_SnowCave.mat` has `Art/Classic/Materials/Environment/Mat_SnowCave_Classic.mat`, `Prefabs/VFX/Drop/RareDrop.prefab` has `Art/Classic/Prefabs/VFX/Drop/RareDrop_Classic.prefab`, `Rendering/URPSettings/UniversalRP-HighQuality.asset` has `Art/Classic/Rendering/URPSettings/UniversalRP-HighQuality_Classic.asset` |
| A Classic asset standing for several Anniversary ones | Next to the one it is named after (`SkirtTop_Classic.mat` with `SkirtTop.mat`, though it also stands for `GlowSkirt.mat`), or else their common folder, its original name followed by `_Classic` (`Art/Classic/Materials/GlowBlue_Classic.mat` for the crystals, Kameiko's energy and the spectral mushrooms) |
| The Classic's own assets (the UI sprites, the objects the Anniversary doesn't have) | The `places` of `pairs.json`: `UI/HUD`, `UI/Inventory` (its `Pieces`), `UI/Menus` (its `Options`), `Prefabs/Entities/Enemies/GreatKameikoSmoke_Classic` |
| The files these need (textures, shaders, VFX graphs) | Their original names, in the folder of what uses them, or in a `Shaders` or `Textures` subfolder of the common folder when several folders use them (`Art/Classic/VFX/Shaders`) |

`pairs.json` lists besides the three pair kinds its `counterparts` (a Classic asset reached otherwise than through `ClassicSkin`, laid out as a pair) and its `places`. The restored shaders keep the `Classic/` prefix of their shader name. `organize.py` moves the file and its `.meta` (the GUID follows, and with it every reference), rewrites the paths naming them (`restored.json`, the USS `url()`, the docs and scripts) and removes the emptied folders; run it again after each restore or new pair, then `build_skin.py`. A second run moves nothing.

### Recipes

**A new VFX of the Anniversary** (a new ability effect, an ambience, a feedback): build it in the Anniversary's folders; the object that holds it gets an `EditionOnly` of the Anniversary (its prefab's root for an effect, the room's `Anniversary` group for level art), or its behaviour goes in the `behaviours` of an existing one. The Classic doesn't show it. `coverage.py` lists its new materials until they are covered: listed in its `KNOWN` with the reason, since the Classic never shows them.

**A VFX of the original redone** (the same ability, a new look): its materials got new shaders or values, so the Classic needs the original's. `material_diff.py` lists them; `python restore.py <the material's path on main>` (`--force` if its file is unchanged but its shader was rewritten), add the `[anniversary, main:…]` pairs in `materials` of `pairs.json`, `python organize.py`, `python build_skin.py`, compile, then check the Classic materials load without shader errors. If the prefab itself changed shape, pair the prefab in `prefabs` instead (restored the same way) when code instantiates it through `VFXPool` or `EditionSkin.Resolve`, or hide the new objects with an `EditionOnly`.

**An animation redone**: keep the original clip. If the new clip is a new file (an ability's `animationClip` or `followUpClip` set to it, a new clip in an entity's `.overrideController`), add `[new clip, old clip]` to `clips` of `pairs.json`. If the clip file is edited in place (an `.anim` or a model's clip reimported), first `restore.py` the original from main (it lands in `Art/Classic` under a new GUID), then pair the edited clip with the restored one. A clip in a model is written `Assets/…/Model.fbx#<fileID>`, its file ID from the model's `.meta` (`internalIDToNameTable`, `74:` entries). Then `organize.py` and `build_skin.py`: `EntityAnimator` swaps the clips on `Edition.Changed`, even mid-game. A new animation event method must work with both clips (the original's has none of the new events), and an ability's timings (`impactDelay`) are shared by both editions: when the new clip's rhythm changes them, the original clip plays with the new timings unless the Classic's profile sets them. `coverage.py` lists the clips new or changed since main that no pair covers.

**A new UI element or animation**: see the table above; the Classic's sprites go under `Art/Classic/UI/<HUD|Inventory|Menus>` with a `places` entry if restored from main.

### Migrations

- **Renaming or moving** an asset keeps its GUID: `ClassicSkin` and the `EditionOnly` lists follow. `pairs.json` names paths: run `build_skin.py --check` after a move, and fix the paths it can't resolve. After renaming or moving an Anniversary asset that has a Classic side, once its path is fixed in `pairs.json`, `organize.py` moves and renames the Classic side after it.
- **Restructuring a prefab** (moving a component, splitting an object): the `EditionOnly` lists reference components and objects by file ID; find them with `unity-asset-refs` before, and retarget them like any other reference (`unity-yaml-edit`).
- **Removing a system**: remove its entries from the `EditionOnly` lists and its profile settings from both profiles in the same commit.
- **A Classic asset breaking** (a Unity or package upgrade, a shader that no longer compiles): fix the copy under `Art/Classic`, never `main`. `restore.py` only brings an asset once; delete its entry from `restored.json` to take it again from main.
- **A new room or a room rebuilt**: `room_diff.py` gives its differences with main, its level art of the Anniversary goes in its `Anniversary` group and the objects of the original shown back in its `Classic` group, each listed by an `EditionOnly` of the root (`RoomTidy` files them).

### Performance

- **Nothing hidden may keep running**: prefer deactivating objects to disabling components; stop particle systems (`particleSystems`) rather than only hiding their renderer; a disabled component clears its property blocks (they keep the renderers out of the SRP Batcher) and its global shader values.
- **No per-frame edition logic**: `EditionMaterials` walks the renderers only when a scene, room, enemy or pooled VFX is created, and on a switch (in the Anniversary, only while some renderer still holds Classic materials to give back); the profile settings are read on events, not every frame; `Edition.Profile` itself is a cached reference, dropped when the edition changes, so reading it in a loop costs a field read.
- **Memory**: `GameAssets` is loaded from `Resources` and references `ClassicSkin`, so the Classic's materials and textures are loaded in both editions (a few MB). If that grows, move the Classic's pairs to Addressables loaded on the switch.
- **Build size**: `Art/Classic` adds the original's textures and shaders; only what a pair or a Classic object references is built.

## Rules

- A new visual, effect, ambience or UX aid of the Anniversary says what it becomes in the Classic: marked Anniversary only with `EditionOnly` (the default: the original didn't have it), or paired in `ClassicSkin`. `coverage.py` of the `classic-restore` skill lists what is left.
- Never test the edition in a view, an action or a gameplay system: use a setting of `EditionProfile`, an `EditionOnly` or a pair.
- The Classic assets restored from `main` never overwrite the Anniversary ones: many kept their original GUID with a new content, so a restored copy with a taken GUID gets a new one.
